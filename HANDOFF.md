## Changes since BUILD_PROMPT was written (these win)
- **Free-spin starts: Dare 8, Double Dare 10, Triple Dare 10, Hidden 10.** +2 per newly completed stack, cap 18 spins.
- **Dare:** wilds stay locked during a spin's respins, then ALL clear after that respin set – unless a full stack forms
  in those respins, which upgrades the bonus to Double Dare once (+2 spins) and locks every wild from then on.
- **Hidden** is never buyable; it triggers naturally in base/Ante (≥2 full-reel wilds) and via Mystery.
- **Mystery Spin:** odds 50% none / 37% Double Dare / 10% Triple Dare / 3% Hidden, decided before the reveal.
  The tease is a normal spin with a wild in a winning line (any reel); the respins are the reveal. The reveal only
  decides the tier: the bonus then starts fresh on Mystery's own reels with that tier's wilds. Ignore the
  "partial-stack reel" anticipation wording in BUILD_PROMPT §5 item 8.
- **Math is not final yet.** It is being retuned (all 7 modes) and will land in `math/games/elfhaus/` on `main`
  with `library/publish_files/` and sample books. Until then build against EVENTS.md + the fixture books and keep
  `sync-config.mjs` reading costs/RTP from `index.json` + stats so the numbers drop in automatically.
  **Do not edit anything under `math/`.**

## UI rules from the studio owner (must follow)
- Palette: greys and purples only for frame, tiles, panels, UI. **No browns, tans, wood or cream.**
- Matte, painted, shaded look (like Lilith's Curse). **No neon, no glows, no glossy gradients, nothing that looks AI-generated.**
  No glow around wilds – just a subtle animation.
- Reel grid: every cell is its own box (single cells respin), boxes butt together with **no gaps** between reels/rows.
- Type: titles in a sharp, beveled serif (like the Waylanders Forge logo). **No bubbly fonts.**
- Controls minimal like Waylanders Forge: dark translucent bar with white line icons (menu, minus, round spin, plus,
  autoplay), plain balance / play / win strip, round icon buttons for turbo and the bonus buy (simple logo icon).
- Content: adults only ("All characters are adults." in rules); never nipples or genitals.
